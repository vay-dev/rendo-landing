You know how it usually goes.

The house hunt is not the hard part. Knowing who to trust with a year's rent is.

On Rendo: the owner and the title papers are checked before the home is listed. You pay a viewing deposit, not a fee, and it always comes back: off your rent, or into your wallet.

How renting works on Rendo

From the first viewing to the keys, in five steps.

  1. Find a checked home

    Browse without an account. Every home has a checked owner behind it.

  2. Book a viewing

    Pick a time. The agent confirms it, and you see the home in person.

    Deposit comes back to you

  3. Apply and get an offer

    Send one application. The landlord replies with an offer you can accept or negotiate.

  4. Pay through Rendo

    Pay by bank transfer. Rendo holds the money, part by part, until handover.

    Held until handover

  5. Sign and move in

    A lawyer draws up the agreement, you both sign on Rendo, and you get the keys.

What you pay, before you pay

Every naira has a job, and you see it first.

Your move-in payment is split into parts. Each one is shown before you pay, and each one is paid out only when its job is done.

Held until handover

Your caution deposit is recorded on a certificate. Anything taken from it at the end is itemised, and you can dispute what you did not cause.

Your money back if it falls through

If either side cancels before handover, everything you paid is refunded.

Questions

What people ask before they rent.

Is Rendo an agent?

No. Rendo is where the landlord, the agent, the lawyer and you meet. We check the people and the papers, hold the money, and keep the record of the let. An agent may still show you the home.

Who holds my money?

Rendo holds it, part by part, until the handover is complete. The landlord is paid once you have the keys. Your caution deposit stays held for the whole tenancy.

What does it cost me?

A viewing deposit when you book a viewing. Nobody keeps it: it comes off your rent if you take the home, and goes back to your wallet if you do not. Then the move-in payment shown on your offer: rent, legal fee, caution, and any agency fee or service charge the offer lists. Nothing is added later.

Why should I get vetted?

Vetting is an identity check, done once in your profile. Vetted renters pay a smaller viewing deposit, and landlords see the Vetted badge when you book, so you are taken seriously from the first viewing.

What if the home is not as listed?

Do not complete the handover. If either side cancels before handover, everything you paid comes back to you.

What is the caution certificate?

A record of your caution deposit, issued at handover. At the end of the tenancy any deduction is listed item by item, and you can dispute what you did not cause.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes, if you work at one of Rendo's partner companies. Confirm your work email in your profile and we check with your employer.
