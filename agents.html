What Rendo solves for agents

The work is the viewing. The chasing should not be.

Every agent knows these four. Each one has an answer built in.

"I'll settle you after."

The tenant pays the landlord, and your fee turns into a phone call.

On Rendo: your fee is its own part of the move-in payment. The renter sees it before they pay, and it is released when the handover is complete.

"They did not show."

A Saturday of viewings, and half the renters never came.

On Rendo: renters pay a viewing deposit. You see a Vetted badge and how many viewings they missed before you confirm a time.

"Is this landlord real?"

A listing from someone who may not own the house.

On Rendo: the landlord confirms who they are and uploads the title document before the listing goes live. Rendo checks both.

"Who drafts the agreement?"

Paperwork between you, the landlord and a lawyer you have to find.

On Rendo: a lawyer reviews the terms and draws up the agreement. You and both sides sign on Rendo.

Ground rules

Straight terms, in plain words.

Rendo's agents, like drivers on a ride app.

You work on the Rendo platform under its standards. A landlord can pause or remove you on one property. Only Rendo removes an agent from the platform.

One fee, shown before payment.

The renter pays one agency fee, at most 10% of the yearly rent. Every agent's rate is their own, and it is visible on the offer.

You work with the landlord's permission.

The landlord decides what you can do on their property: find tenants, manage the tenancy, or sign for them. Nothing happens on a property until you accept it.

Questions

What agents ask before they join.

Who pays my fee?

Usually the renter, as one part of the move-in payment. An offer can also say the landlord pays it. Either way it is written on the offer before anyone pays.

When do I get paid?

The renter pays through Rendo and the money is held until the handover is complete. Your fee is released as part of that.

Can I list a property for a landlord?

Yes. You name the landlord, and they confirm who they are, upload the title document and tick what you may do. Rendo checks the papers before the listing goes live.

What if a renter does not show up?

You mark it as "They did not come". It counts against that renter, and every landlord and agent sees the count next to their name.

Can I choose my own fee?

Yes, up to 10% of the yearly rent. You can set one rate for letting, and a separate one if you sell.

Who writes the agreement?

A lawyer reviews the offer terms and draws up the agreement. You do not have to find one. A landlord can also bring their own verified lawyer.
